# Hatchery — Player Guide

Welcome to **OldSchool breeding**! This is the classic, ranch-style Pixelmon
breeding loop: set up a Day Care, give it a good home, and hatch eggs the
way it used to be. This guide is everything a player needs — no commands or
config knowledge required.

---

## 1. Get a Day Care block

Hatchery uses Pixelmon **Day Care** blocks (any of the 16 colours). Obtain one
however your server provides it (crafting, shop, crate, etc.), then **place it
down**. The moment you place it, it becomes *your* daycare.

- You have a **limit** on how many daycares you can own (your rank may raise it).
- Some worlds may have daycares **disabled** — you'll be told if so.
- Break the block to remove the daycare (you may get your Upgrade item back if
  the server enables drop-on-break).

## 2. Add a pair

**Right-click your Day Care** to open the menu, then **Add Pokémon** to pick two
from your party. They must be **compatible**:

- Same/overlapping **egg group**
- Opposite **genders** (or a valid **Ditto** pairing)
- A **discovered** species (no mystery/undiscovered Pokémon)

If they don't match you'll see *"These Pokémon are not compatible."* Swap one
out and try again.

## 3. Build a good environment

This is the heart of OldSchool breeding. Hatchery looks at the **blocks around
your Day Care** and scores them based on your pair's **types**. Match the
habitat to the Pokémon:

- 🔥 **Fire** types love netherrack, magma, lava, fire.
- 💧 **Water** types love water, kelp, prismarine, coral.
- 🌿 **Grass** types love grass, leaves, apricorn logs.
- ⚡ **Electric** → lightning rods, redstone/copper blocks.
- ❄️ **Ice** → ice / packed ice / blue ice / snow.
- …and so on for all 18 types (plus some universal bonuses).

More (and better-matched) blocks = more **environment points**.

## 4. Satisfaction & speed

Your points put the daycare into a **satisfaction tier**, which sets how fast
breeding progresses:

| Tier | Feeling | Speed |
|------|---------|-------|
| Unhappy | 😞 | 0.5× (slow) |
| Content | 😐 | 1.0× |
| Happy | 🙂 | 1.5× |
| Ecstatic | 😄 | 2.0× |
| Blissful | 🤩 | 3.0× (fastest) |

*(Exact thresholds/speeds are server-configurable.)* Open the menu and choose
**View Environment Points** to see your current score and tier, then improve the
surroundings to climb tiers.

## 5. Collect your egg

When progress fills up you'll get *"An egg is ready!"*. Right-click the Day
Care and hit **Collect Egg** — the egg goes to you, ready to hatch the normal
Pixelmon way (walk it / use it per your server's hatching rules).

## 6. Speed boosts

- **Hourglasses** (Bronze / Silver / Gold) — right-click your Day Care while
  holding one to instantly **advance breeding ticks** (Bronze +10, Silver +25,
  Gold +50 by default). They're consumed on use.
- **Environment Upgrade** item — right-click your Day Care to apply it and
  **expand the scan radius**, so more surrounding blocks count. Stacks up to a
  configured maximum.

## 7. Commands you'll use

- `/daycares` (or `/dc`) — list/open your daycares.

That's it — everything else is point-and-click in the GUI.

---

### Quick tips

- A tightly-themed habitat beats a big messy one — matched blocks are weighted
  by type.
- Upgrades widen the radius; combine with a themed build for top tiers.
- Save Gold Hourglasses for rare/long pairings.
- Your rank may grant **more daycares** — run more pairs in parallel.

Happy hatching! 🥚
