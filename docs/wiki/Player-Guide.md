# Player Guide

The non-technical guide — share this with your community. (Full version also in
the repo at `docs/PLAYER_GUIDE.md`.)

## 1. Get & place a Day Care

Obtain a Pixelmon **Day Care** block (any of 16 colours) however your server
gives them, and place it. It instantly becomes **your** daycare.
- You have a **daycare limit** (your rank may raise it).
- Some worlds disable daycares — you'll be told.
- Breaking it removes the daycare.

## 2. Add a compatible pair

Right-click → **Add Pokémon** → pick two from your **party**. They must be
compatible: same/overlapping **egg group**, opposite **gender** (or a valid
**Ditto**), and **discovered**. Otherwise: *"These Pokémon are not compatible."*

## 3. Build the right habitat

Hatchery scores the **blocks around the daycare** against your pair's **types**:

- 🔥 Fire → netherrack, magma, lava, fire
- 💧 Water → water, kelp, prismarine, coral
- 🌿 Grass → grass, leaves, apricorn logs
- ⚡ Electric → lightning rods, redstone/copper
- ❄️ Ice → ice / packed / blue ice / snow
- …all 18 types (+ universal bonuses)

Better-matched blocks = more points.

## 4. Satisfaction = speed

| Tier | Speed |
|------|-------|
| Unhappy | 0.5× |
| Content | 1.0× |
| Happy | 1.5× |
| Ecstatic | 2.0× |
| Blissful | 3.0× |

Use **View Environment Points** in the menu to see your score/tier, then improve
the surroundings.

## 5. Collect the egg

When it's ready you'll be told — right-click → **Collect Egg**. Hatch it the
normal Pixelmon way.

## 6. Speed boosts

- **Hourglasses** (Bronze/Silver/Gold) — right-click the daycare holding one to
  jump ticks (consumed on use).
- **Environment Upgrade** — right-click to widen the scan radius (stacks to a
  max).

## Commands

- `/daycares` (`/dc`) — list/open your daycares.

## Tips

- Tight, themed builds beat big messy ones.
- Upgrades + dense theme = top tiers.
- Save Gold Hourglasses for long/rare pairs.
- Higher ranks may run more daycares in parallel.

Happy hatching! 🥚
